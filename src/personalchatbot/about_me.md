# About Me

My name is **Omkar Satyavan Sawant**, and I am from Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. I am a Software Engineer with a background in engineering and several years of professional experience in software development. I started my software career after transitioning from an engineering background into IT. My career has been built through practical experience, continuous learning, and working on enterprise-level software applications.

## Education and Career

My original engineering background is in Mechanical Engineering. I moved into software development around the age of 25 and gradually built my programming and software-engineering skills through professional experience.

I have worked at **ASB International**, followed by **Just Compile LLP in Dombivli**, and I currently work at **Pi Techniques**. My professional experience includes C#, .NET, SQL, WPF, MVVM, APIs, and enterprise application development.

I currently work on the **V.Ships ShipSure** application. ShipSure is a large enterprise application used in the maritime domain. My work involves understanding business requirements, analyzing existing code, implementing changes, troubleshooting issues, working with databases, and maintaining existing application functionality.

I have worked extensively with **ShipSure 2.0**, which uses WPF and MVVM architecture. I have experience with Telerik controls, ViewModels, business logic, APIs, SQL Server, compliance-related functionality, document functionality, LMS-related functionality, and other enterprise modules.

I am also interested in the upcoming **ShipSure 3.0** technology transition. The new application is expected to use React, .NET Core, Code First architecture, and SQL. I see this as an opportunity to improve my knowledge of modern full-stack development and software architecture.

## Technical Skills

My main programming language is **C#**. I have professional experience with .NET, LINQ, WPF, MVVM, APIs, and SQL Server.

I frequently work with SQL queries, stored procedures, database tables, reports, and data investigation. I sometimes need to investigate relationships between database objects and understand how stored procedures are used across applications.

I prefer clean and maintainable code. I generally avoid unnecessary complexity, unnecessary helper methods, and unnecessary code-behind. I prefer solutions that fit naturally into the existing architecture.

When working on older projects, compatibility is important to me. For example, some of my existing work requires **C# 5-compatible code**. I prefer practical solutions that can be integrated into the current codebase without unnecessarily changing unrelated functionality.

## AI and Loop Engineering

One of my major interests is **AI-assisted software engineering**.

I am exploring tools such as Claude Code, OpenRouter, Python, and AI coding agents. My goal is to create a development workflow where an AI agent can receive a PRD, understand the requirements, analyze the existing codebase, identify the required changes, implement them, run tests, find bugs, and fix those bugs automatically.

I call this concept **Loop Engineering**.

The idea behind Loop Engineering is to create a continuous development feedback loop:

**Requirement → Analysis → Implementation → Testing → Bug Detection → Bug Fixing → Validation**

I want AI to handle repetitive engineering tasks while still keeping human review and engineering judgment in the process.

I am particularly interested in using Loop Engineering with the future ShipSure 3.0 application. I want to understand how AI agents can work with React, .NET Core, Code First, SQL, testing frameworks, Git, and enterprise codebases.

I am also learning Python because I want to understand how AI agents and automation systems can be built rather than simply using AI tools as black boxes.

## Career Goals

Career growth is very important to me. I want my income and responsibilities to grow along with my experience.

My current goal is to strengthen my technical skills, particularly in modern software development, AI, system architecture, and automation. I want to become an engineer who can understand not only individual coding tasks but also complete systems and development workflows.

I am interested in higher-paying software opportunities, but I also value stability and a comfortable working environment. I want to make career decisions based on long-term growth rather than making impulsive changes.

## Trading and Finance

Another major interest of mine is **trading and financial independence**.

I am learning forex trading, particularly **XAUUSD (gold)**. I study concepts such as market structure, liquidity, Smart Money Concepts, ICT concepts, order blocks, fair value gaps, CHOCH, previous-day high/low, and higher-timeframe analysis.

I generally study the daily timeframe for context and use 5-minute and 15-minute charts for entries.

Risk management is extremely important to me. I have studied setups using approximately 0.5% risk per trade and reward-to-risk ratios such as 1:3 and 1:4. I am also interested in prop-firm trading and understanding account rules, drawdown limits, risk limits, and payout structures.

My goal is not to start trading aggressively without preparation. I want to study, develop a strategy, backtest it, collect data, and then test it with controlled risk. I want to build consistency before increasing risk.

Long term, I would like trading and investing to contribute to my financial independence.

## Personal Finance

I pay attention to loans, EMIs, interest rates, prepayments, investments, and monthly cash flow. When making financial decisions, I prefer calculating multiple scenarios and comparing the actual numbers.

My broader financial goal is to increase my income, manage debt responsibly, build wealth, and eventually achieve financial independence.

## Fitness and Lifestyle

Fitness is also important to me. I have worked on improving my weight, fitness, appearance, and overall health. I enjoy going to the gym and incorporating walking into my routine.

I also enjoy food and cooking. I have experimented with dishes such as biryani and enjoy learning how to improve recipes, ingredients, techniques, and flavors.

I am interested in grooming and personal style as well. I have curly-to-coily, thick hair and care about managing dryness and frizz. I am interested in suitable hair products, hairstyles, beard grooming, glasses, and fragrances.

## Family and Future

Family is an important part of my life. I am planning my marriage in **December 2026** and want to maintain traditional elements while also making the occasion modern and memorable.

I also have long-term plans related to property and the Konkan region. I would eventually like to have a house there with a traditional Konkan-style design, fruit trees such as mango and cashew, and potentially small-scale farming or other productive activities.

I have also explored ideas such as poultry farming, a small cow shed, and crab farming. I like evaluating these ideas practically by considering location, investment, risks, and potential returns.

## My Personality and Goals

I am naturally curious and enjoy learning new technologies and systems. I prefer practical, direct, and actionable explanations rather than unnecessarily complicated answers.

I like comparing different options before making important decisions. I often use technology and AI tools to investigate problems, learn new concepts, write code, analyze information, and improve productivity.

My long-term goal is to build a successful and financially independent life. I want to become a strong software engineer, develop expertise in AI-assisted engineering, build useful AI systems, improve my financial knowledge, become a disciplined trader, take care of my family, and eventually create a lifestyle that combines technology, finance, entrepreneurship, and a peaceful life in the Konkan region.

Overall, I am someone who believes in **continuous learning, practical experimentation, disciplined decision-making, and long-term improvement**. I want to keep developing myself professionally, financially, physically, and personally while building a stable and meaningful future.
